Privacy Policy for Florida Fishing Guide

Last updated: 2026

DuxByte ("we", "us") makes the Florida Fishing Guide app ("the app"). This page explains what information the app uses and why.

What the app uses

Your location. With your permission (optional), the app reads your phone's approximate or precise location while you are using the app. You can also choose a spot on the map instead. It is used to show tides, weather, and conditions for where you are. To do that, the app sends your coordinates to public government services: the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) for tides and the National Weather Service (NWS) for weather. We do not receive, store, or sell your location on any server of ours.

To show a readable place name (for example "Palmetto, FL"), the app asks your phone's built-in location service to turn your coordinates into a place name. On Android this service is provided by Google.

Maps. The in-app map loads map images from U.S. government servers (NOAA and USGS) and the map library from a content delivery network (cdnjs). These servers can see the area you are viewing and your device's IP address, as with any website. You can use the app without turning on location: you can choose spots by moving a pin on the map.

Saved fishing spots and catch log. Spots and catches you save (names, notes, coordinates, and the tide, weather, and moon conditions at the time) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. If you delete the app, they are deleted.

Purchases. If you buy Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We use a service called RevenueCat to confirm that a purchase is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information (for example, which plan you bought and whether it is active). We do not receive your payment card details. See the RevenueCat and Google Play privacy policies for how they handle data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or ask for your name or email.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not sell your personal information.
  • It does not track your location in the background.

Your choices

You can turn off location permission at any time in your phone settings. The app will then show conditions for a default Florida location. You can delete your saved spots inside the app, or by uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Not official fishing regulations

Fishing rules shown in the app are for convenience only and are not an official source. Always check the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC) for the current rules.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
